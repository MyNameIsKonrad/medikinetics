# Medikinetics — Architecture

Read on demand: before changing the PK model, rendering, storage, the service worker, or anything a Decision Log row names. `AGENTS.md` holds the rules every session needs; this file holds how the app works, how it got here, and why.

Line references are to `index.html` unless noted, as of 2026-09-22. They drift, so search by name.

## PK model

- One-compartment oral absorption (Bateman equation) in `phaseConc`. Elimination `KE = 0.347 /h` (t½ ≈ 2 h) is shared by every med.
- Each `MEDS` entry is `{ label, sublabel, totalMg, color, phases, fastedPhases?, shortLabel? }`. `phases` is a list of `{ offsetHours, mg, durationHours, ka }`, and a dose's curve is the sum of its phases. `totalMg` is set by hand, not derived from the phases; it drives "taken today", export mg and labels. `shortLabel` is what a log row shows when present. All phase iteration goes through `phasesFor(pill)`, which picks `fastedPhases` over `phases` when the type has them and the pill is not `fed`.
- `phaseConc` handles the equal-rate limit (`ka ≈ KE`) with its closed form instead of dividing by zero (PR #19).
- **Two phase kinds.** A bolus phase (`phaseConc`) releases its `mg` at once. A zero-order phase has `windowHours` and releases its `mg` at a constant rate over `[offsetHours, offsetHours + windowHours]`, each slice absorbed first-order at `ka`. `zeroOrderConc` is `phaseConc` integrated over the window: with `u = min(t, W)` and `I(c) = (e^(-c(t-u)) - e^(-c·t)) / c`, `C = NORM · mg/W · ka/(ka-KE) · (I(KE) - I(ka))`, plus its own equal-rate branch. A near-zero window reduces to the bolus. `phaseConcFor(ph, tH)` dispatches; both concentration paths (`concAtTime`, `buildCurve`) use it. On zero-order phases `durationHours` mirrors `windowHours` so the card shows the release window.
- **Concerta** (`concerta(mg)` factory → `CON72` / `CON36` / `CON18`): 22% bolus overcoat (display window 1h), then 20% over 1–4h, 36% over 4–7h, 22% over 7–12h, all ka 2.0. Fractions follow the FDA biopharmaceutics review's in-vivo cumulative release (≈20% at 2h, 30% at 4h, 55% at 6h, 70% at 8h, read off a plot) and label §11.1 (22% overcoat, rising release rate over 6–7h). Checked numerically at 72mg: peak 28.8 mg eq at 7.2h (label: Tmax 6.8 ± 1.8h, adults); the 1h value is 55% of peak (label Fig 1: ~57%; Markowitz 2003: 58%); AUC equals 72mg of IR (fractions sum to 1.0); the card clears (10% of its own peak, Decision #37) at ≈17.5h. Dose-proportional at 18–144mg (label §12.3), so one shape scales. Sources: FDA label 2023 (accessdata.fda.gov, NDA 021121), FDA NDA 21-121 biopharmaceutics review (2000), Markowitz 2003 (PMID 12648029), Atenza SmPC (rejestry.ezdrowie.gov.pl, product 41244). (verified: node check of the shipped formulas, 2026-09-22)
- **"mg eq" is IR-peak-equivalent, not mg in the body.** `NORM` scales every phase so that 1 mg of IR (`ka = KA_REF = 2.0`) peaks at exactly 1.0; 10 mg IR peaks at 10.0 mg eq. The one shared `NORM` keeps every med on a single comparable scale. A slower `ka` gives a lower, later, wider peak for the same total exposure (area under the curve ≈ 41.6 per 10 mg at every `ka` tested, 0.2–2.0). That is correct pharmacokinetics for equal bioavailability, not a scaling bug. Per-`ka` normalization is ruled out: it would inflate slow meds' peaks and break comparison between meds. (verified: numeric check of `phaseConc` during the 2026-09-22 audit; answers issue #20, closed by the housekeeping PR)
- `durationHours` is a display parameter, not a PK constant. It drives phase status and progress bars (`pillCardBodyHTML`), card visibility (`pillIsVisible`) and the chart window's right edge (`getCurveWindow`). CR fasted uses `durationHours: 6`.

## Data and storage

- `localStorage['medikinetics-v1']` holds an array of `{ id, type, takenAt, fed? }`. `id` is `Date.now()` at log time (not `takenAt`); `fed` exists only on types that have `fastedPhases`.
- The full history is kept forever; every display and math path filters through `last24h()`. **`loadPills` drops any stored dose whose `type` is not a key in `MEDS`, and the next `savePills` writes the filtered array back.** Removing or renaming a `MEDS` key therefore erases that med's history. (verified: `loadPills`, line ~488)
- `localStorage['medikinetics-feel-v1']` holds check-ins `{ id, at, feel }`, newest first, where `feel` is a `FEELS` key (`on`, `fading`, `off`). `loadFeels` drops entries with an unknown `feel` or a non-positive `at`, as `loadPills` does for doses. Kept forever. A check-in belongs to the most recent dose at or before it, within 24 h (`doseBefore`), and its "after" time counts from that dose (`afterDose`).
- `exportData()` shares the full history as enriched JSON (mg, ISO time) through the Web Share API, falling back to a blob download. Since 2.0 the file also carries `checkins` (`{ feel, at, time }`); `doses` keeps its shape (Decision #46). `importData()` reads such a file back, additive-only (Decision #45). The footer is always shown, counts stored doses and check-ins, and hides only the export button while nothing is stored.
- Undo is single-level with an 8 s window (`UNDO_DURATION_MS`) for either record kind (`stageUndo`); a second delete replaces the pending undo, and restore re-sorts by time.

## Rendering

- `render()` runs on load, on every mutation except the fed toggle (Decision #3), on resize (150 ms debounce), and on a 30 s interval. The interval is cleared while the page is hidden and restarted with an immediate render on return (`visibilitychange`).
- Dose buttons are built once from every non-`retired` `MEDS` entry, in declaration order, into a two-column grid (`#dose-grid`). With an odd count the first (main) button gets `.dose-btn--wide` and spans the row, so nothing is stranded; today that is Concerta 72 on top, 36 and 18 below. There is no debounce: after a real log every `.dose-btn` gets `disabled` + `.just-fired` (opacity .35) for 400 ms, and the `touchstart` press-feedback handler skips disabled elements, so feedback and action stay in sync.
- Pill cards carry `data-id="${pill.id}"`, which `toggleFed()` uses to find the card it updates in place. Log rows color their "clearing" status with `--clearing: #614f8a`; the card's own clearing line uses `--soft`.
- Dose time: offset chips `-30m`, `-1h`, `-2h`, `-3h`, `now` (default), plus an exact-time chip. The same when-row times doses and check-ins (`selectedTime`, Decision #42). A typed time later than now means yesterday at that time (`timestampForTimeInput`). While the exact chip is selected, the "log" label shows the day and time the entry will land on (`updateDoseWhen`, e.g. "· yesterday 23:10"); it sits outside `#offset-row`, so it updates freely without touching the picker (Decision #39). After each real log the selection resets to `now` (`afterLog`). `render()` skips rebuilding `#offset-row` while the time input is focused or `_timeInputActive` is set, so the native iOS picker survives the 30 s tick.
- Feel chips (`#feel-row`, "feels: on / fading / off") are built once, like the dose grid, between the dose grid and the when-row. After any log, dose buttons and feel chips are disabled together for 400 ms.
- Check-ins inside the chart window sit on the total curve at their time (`feelShape`: filled = on, half = fading, hollow = off, in `--feel`, Decision #41). Their fingerprint is part of `_lastSvgKey`, so a new or deleted check-in redraws the SVG even when the curve is unchanged.
- The 24 h log interleaves dose rows and check-in rows by time, newest first. A check-in row shows its time after the dose.
- Chart window (`getCurveWindow`): start = min(earliest dose since local midnight − 45 min, now − 60 min); end = max(last phase end of any 24 h dose + 4 h, now + 90 min). With no doses: now − 30 min to now + 8 h.
- Chart: 10-minute sampling; y-axis floor of 5 mg eq with 1.2× headroom; x-ticks every 1, 2 or 4 h by window length. `MEDS.IR.color` is the total-curve, gradient and tooltip-ring color.
- Two caches: `buildCurve` single-slot memo keyed on the pill fingerprint and window, and `_lastSvgKey`, which skips the SVG rebuild when the curve key, width and now-pixel are unchanged.
- Tooltip/scrub handlers attach once to `#chart-container` and read the shared `_chart` object. Scrubbing a past point writes `scrubTime` and pre-fills the time chip but does not select it; scrubbing ahead of now only shows the projected level (Decision #38). The tooltip hides 2.5 s after touchend.
- No dose is ever in the future: a typed time later than now means yesterday, a scrub fills the chip only with past times, and `logPill` clamps `takenAt` to now. The ephemeral dose preview was removed (Decision #38).
- Cards stay visible while a phase is running or concentration is at least `clearingLevel(pill)`: `CLEARING_THRESHOLD` (10%) of the dose's own peak (`peakFor`, sampled once per type and food state). After the last phase ends they show "clearing" with a time-left estimate from pure elimination to that level. Log rows fade at the same point (both go through `pillIsVisible`); the graph and "in system" keep every 24 h dose, tails included (Decision #37). Progress bars drain from full to empty.
- Card lifetimes at 10% of peak: IR 8.2 h, CR fasted 9.5 h, CR fed and Symkinet 14 h, Concerta 17.5 h (every strength). (verified: node check of the shipped `pillIsVisible`, 2026-09-22)
- The stats line: "taken today" counts since local midnight; "in system" sums the rolling 24 h; "↑ rising" compares now against `now + RISING_LOOKAHEAD_MS`.

## History section

- `renderHistory` lists every day from today back to the earliest dose or check-in, newest first: `HISTORY_FIRST` (7) days at first, `HISTORY_STEP` (30) more per "show more days". A run of days with nothing logged collapses into one line (Decision #44).
- Each day strip draws the model's total for that local day on a 00–24 axis at 15-minute samples, including doses from the 24 h before it, so a late dose's tail carries into the next day. Every strip shares one y-scale: `dayCurves` computes every day, not just those shown, so the scale does not jump on "show more". Dose ticks sit on the baseline in the med's color; check-ins sit on the curve.
- Caching: `_historyKey` (the day, days shown, width, and the full dose and check-in fingerprint) skips the rebuild on the 30 s tick; `dayCurves` is keyed on the day and the dose fingerprint, so a new day gets its curve at midnight. `toggleFed` calls `renderHistory` too; it touches no card, so Decision #3 holds.
- "How long it lasts" (`lastsSummaryHTML`): for each day with a dose of a non-retired med, the first check-in of each kind, measured from the most recent dose; medians grouped by that day's non-retired total mg, with the number of days behind each value. Hidden until at least one such check-in exists (Decision #43).

## Service worker and releases

- **Version stamping is CI's job.** `.github/workflows/version-stamp.yml` runs on every push to `main` (skipping its own commits) and rewrites `VERSION` in `sw.js` and the `#version-label` fallback in `index.html` to `YYYYMMDD.NNNN` (date of the head commit, workflow run number). It commits as `github-actions[bot]` straight to `main` — the one sanctioned exception to "never push to main". Local `main` is one commit behind after every merge; pull before branching.
- The client shows the "update available · RELOAD" banner when an activating worker broadcasts a version different from the label (Decision #21).
- `sw.js`: install precaches `index.html` (required) plus the manifest, the three fonts and the four icons (other failures are swallowed and backfilled at runtime). Navigation is stale-while-revalidate, so a new `index.html` shows on the launch after the one that fetched it. `skipWaiting` + `clients.claim`; old caches are deleted on activate; an inline offline fallback page covers a cold offline launch; offline misses get a 504.
- Platform handling in `index.html`: `prefers-reduced-motion` disables transitions and animations; `contain: layout style` on cards and log rows; a `<noscript>` message; iOS standalone meta tags and safe-area insets; a 150 ms debounced resize redraw.
